ABOUT SCORELINE

ScoreLine is a football match prediction platform built to forecast full-match outcomes—estimating the probability of a home win, a draw, or an away win—rather than individual player stat lines.

Platform Scope

BUILT FOR MATCH-LEVEL INSIGHT

ScoreLine evaluates historical match results from Europe's top five leagues (Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A and Ligue 1) to calculate clear pre-match outcome probabilities. Every prediction splits 100% across the three possible match results: Home Win, Draw, and Away Win.

Rather than predicting individual player performance metrics like goals or assists, ScoreLine incorporates overall player Elo ratings as an input signal alongside team strength and recent form to measure overall squad quality before kick-off.

HOW ARE THESE PREDICTIONS MADE?

ScoreLine evaluates past results from the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A and Ligue 1 and works out how likely each outcome is: a home win, a draw, or an away win. The three percentages always add up to 100%.

What Is Captured in the Model

Team Strength Rating

Every team has a rating that goes up when it wins and down when it loses. Beating a strong team earns more than beating a weak one. The bigger the gap between the two teams, the more the model favours the stronger side.

Overall Player Elo Rating

ScoreLine incorporates overall player Elo ratings as a model input to reflect underlying squad calibre. Rather than predicting individual player stats, these ratings help inform the overall match outcome forecast.

Recent Form

Tracks how many goals each team has been scoring and conceding in their last few games. Recent matches count more than older ones so current momentum is reflected accurately.

Expected Scorelines

A goals model estimates how many goals each team is likely to score against this particular opponent, then works out how often the match would end in a home win, draw, or away win.

Turning Information Into a Prediction

ScoreLine has learned from three seasons of real results how much each of these features matters. It learns weights during supervised learning and adds them up for each match, giving a probability for each outcome. It only ever uses information from before kick-off, so it never cheats by looking at the actual relevant result.

Performance Validity

The model was trained on older matches and tested on later matches it had never seen. It beats simply guessing the most common result, and its probabilities align well with how often results actually occur across real fixtures in all five leagues.

Keep in Mind

Football is unpredictable. The data tells us draws are especially hard to call, and things like injuries, transfers, or late team news aren't included. A 60% chance of a home win still means the away side avoids defeat about 4 times in 10. ScoreLine predictions are provided for analytical insight, not betting advice.
